### Delivery Performance and Customer Satisfaction

- Analysed **96,470 delivered orders** with valid delivery dates.
- Identified a **6.77% late-delivery rate** among the analysed orders.
- Late-delivered orders had an average customer review score of **2.22/5**, compared with **4.27/5** for orders delivered on time or early.
- Low ratings (1–2 stars) represented **63.24%** of reviewed late-delivery orders, compared with **9.73%** of reviewed on-time or early orders.
- **Business recommendation:** Investigate fulfilment and carrier delays, improve delivery-status communication, and monitor customer satisfaction by delivery performance.

*Note: These findings indicate an association between delivery timeliness and customer satisfaction; they do not establish causation.*
